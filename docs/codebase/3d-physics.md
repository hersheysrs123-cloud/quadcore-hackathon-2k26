# 3D studio — Physics scenes

> Part of the [SocraticOS codebase reference](../../CODEBASE_SUMMARY.md). Refraction, motor effect, lenses, induction, gas laws, projectile, interference, orbits, shadows, incline, Hooke's law, simple machines, roller coaster, circuits, static electricity, buoyancy, heat transfer.

1. **Physics Engine** (`PhysicsCanvas.jsx` & `ShadowLabCanvas.jsx`):
   - **Wave Refraction & Snell's Law** (`refraction`): Multi-medium light ray refraction, critical angle calculation, total internal reflection, Fresnel reflection rays, and lateral displacement.
   - **The Motor Effect & Fleming's Left-Hand Rule** (`motor`): Magnetic field lines ($N 	o S$), the B, I and F vectors, and a realistic human left hand holding the rule. The hand is our own model (`public/models/fleming-hand.glb`, built by `scripts/hand-model` on the arm build's hand skeleton), meshed in the scene's own axes: first finger +X (B), thumb +Y (F), second finger +Z (I). It loads through `usePackedModel` inside a `Suspense`. Reversing the poles or the current turns the hand by a half-turn (`handRotation`), never a mirror, so it stays a left hand. `turnHandPoint` applies the same turn to the fingertip anchors in `fleming-hand-model-meta.js`, so each arrow starts `TIP_GAP` past its fingertip. The hand and arrows sit `HAND_SHIFT` away from the force's side, so the F arrow and its label stay in frame whichever way it points. The scene's extra ambient and directional lights, which used to ride inside the old procedural robot hand, are now at scene level; the skin material is slightly darkened to stay warm under them.
   - **Ray Optics — Lenses & Curved Mirrors** (`lenses`): Convex and concave lenses (refraction) and concave and convex spherical mirrors (reflection), principal optical axis, focal points $F$ and centers of curvature $2F/C$, parallel/focal/pole rays, real/virtual image arrows with adaptive vector arrow scaling, object height slider ($0.5\text{ cm}–3.0\text{ cm}$), toggles for rays, construction extensions, and labels, detailed 3D optical bench rail with sliding carriages, and speed slider hidden via `hideSpeedSlider: true`.
   - **Electromagnets & Electromagnetic Induction** (`induction`, `ElectromagnetRig.jsx` + `lib/electromagnet.js` for the third apparatus): `InductionScene` is keyed by apparatus, so each opens on its own camera (`INDUCTION_VIEWS`). **Electromagnet:** the bar-magnet rig's coil and bobbin (`Bobbin`, now shared by both) driven by the lab kit's bench supply, each drawn loop standing for 25 turns (100–500), with no core, a soft-iron or a steel rod. `solveElectromagnet` gives the centre field (a finite solenoid, μ₀NI/√(L²+4R²), times the rod's apparent gain of ×8 or ×6, saturating), the pole field, the clips it holds (one per 12.5 mT at the pole, up to six) and the poles by the right-hand grip rule. Its `retained` input is history: what the core kept when the current last changed (soft iron 2 %, steel 45 %; only a field above the coercivity rewrites it). The scene holds it per rod and pushes `liveRetained` for the Details panel. The coil passes through a frosted acrylic sheet at axis height (cut away round the coil and the rod, and stopping just in front of the coil so it does not hide the clip test). On it are about 1,500 iron filings that turn to the field where it beats `FILING_THRESHOLD_T` and drift loose without it, and eleven plotting compasses on damped needles that follow the field plus the Earth's (towards −z). There are also field lines traced on the sheet and mirrored about the axis and the coil's middle. `fieldAt` is the exact field of rings of current (elliptic integrals by the AGM): the winding, plus the core's equivalent surface current. Under the right-hand end a scissor jack dips a dish of clips to the pole every 6.9 s, which tops the hanging chain up; clips drop at once when the field falls. A magnified inset shows the core's domains turning in proportion to its magnetisation. Captions say when steel has become a permanent magnet. **The other two:** an AC Dynamo Generator (rotating rectangular copper coil, slip rings, brush contacts, live AC sinusoidal waveform trace) and Bar Magnet & Solenoid Coil (axial sliding bar magnet, transparent acrylic tube, multi-turn copper solenoid, 3D dipole field loops, Lenz's Law opposing field arrow, and stationary zero-EMF verification). Complete with a high-contrast Center-Zero Galvanometer (`LaboratoryGalvanometer`), an incandescent Demonstration Light Bulb (`DemonstrationBulb`) with power-proportional glow ($P \propto \mathcal{E}^{1.6}$), and animated charge flow particles.
   - **Static Electricity & Charge Transfer** (`static_electricity`): Triboelectric charge transfer, Countable $+/-$ electron carriers, Coulomb inverse-square forces ($F = k q_1 q_2 / r^2$), neutral wall polarisation via induction, Van de Graaff generator with repelling aluminum pie pan stack, air humidity leakage, and interactive 3D balloon raycast dragging (`DraggableBalloon`). The wool sweater is our own model (`public/models/sweater.glb`, built by `scripts/sweater-model`): a cream cable-knit crew-neck on a dress form with a turned wooden cap and a tripod stand, drawn by `SweaterModel` in `sweater-model.jsx`. Its stitches are a fragment shader driven by the per-vertex `_KNIT` coordinates, and they fade out when they are too small on screen to draw without shimmer. The sweater's charge signs sit just in front of its chest at `SWEATER.frontZ` from `sweater-model-meta.js`.
   - **Kinetic Gas Laws ($PV=nRT$)** (`gas`): Bounded cylinder with hot/cold kinetic gas particles, moveable piston, and wall collision impulses.
   - **2D Projectile Motion** (`projectile`): Ballistic flight with quadratic drag, ideal parabolic trajectory comparison, tangent velocity $\vec{v}$, and gravity weight $\vec{W}$. The cannon is our own model (`public/models/cannon.glb`, built in Blender by `scripts/cannon-model`, drawn by `cannon-model.jsx`): a bronze barrel that turns to the launch angle about its trunnions, on an oak carriage whose grain is a fragment shader. A Camera choice (`camera`: `overview` / `follow`) drives `FollowCamera` (scene-kit) to follow the ball.
   - **Wave Interference & Double Slits** (`interference`): Wave crests/troughs, coherent slit emitters, double-slit barrier, and screen intensity fringe maxima.
   - **Keplerian Orbits & Gravity Wells** (`orbits`): Gravitational spacetime potential well ($-GM/r$), central massive body, orbiting satellite, and elliptical/hyperbolic orbital trails.
   - **Light, Shadows & Straight Lines** (`shadows`): Primary optical bench simulation powered by `ShadowLabCanvas.jsx` and `lib/shadowOptics.js`. Features draggable bench elements (light source, stand, screen), pinpoint bulb vs wide lamp penumbra/umbra calculations, 20 shapes in two groups (13 solids such as cube, sphere, cylinder, ring, cone and pyramid, plus 7 flat cut-outs: star, heart, cross, arrow, letter T, letter L and letter F; built in `lib/shadowSolids.js`), material transmission (opaque, translucent, transparent), and a 10%–80% resizable sidebar HUD with edge drag bar and corner grip.
